# Leading Datasets for Benchmarking Clinical LLMs on EHRs

| Dataset / Benchmark | Description | Notes & Key Intakes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MIMIC-III / MIMIC-IV** | Single-center (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston), de-identified EHR database containing structured (vitals, lab results, medications, ICD codes) and unstructured (clinical notes, discharge summaries, radiology reports) data for tens of thousands of ICU and hospital admissions. | • **Biased towards critical patients**; primarily reflects ICU population from a single tertiary academic medical center (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center).<br>• Gold-standard substrate for many fine-tuning and evaluation benchmarks (e.g., mortality prediction, length of stay, readmission, clinical NLP). |
| **eICU Collaborative Research Database** | Multi-center intensive care unit database covering over 200,000 admissions across diverse hospitals in the United States. Contains structured clinical data (vitals, lab scores, care plans, diagnoses). | • Multi-center nature mitigates single-hospital bias found in MIMIC, but remains heavily skewed toward **critical/intensive care settings**. |
| **EHRSQL** | A Text-to-SQL evaluation benchmark built on top of MIMIC-III and eICU schemas. Contains natural language clinical questions paired with executable SQL queries. | • Focuses on evaluating an LLM's ability to translate complex clinical natural language questions into database queries.<br>• Inherits structural and population biases from MIMIC and eICU. |
| **MedAgentBench / EHR-Complex** | Agentic benchmark tasks designed on top of EHR datasets (like MIMIC-IV) requiring multi-step database reasoning, longitudinal retrieval, tool use, and clinical SQL synthesis. | • Focuses on multi-turn reasoning and tool use rather than simple single-turn question answering.<br>• Evaluates complex clinical intent (e.g., patient-level longitudinal aggregation vs. population-level queries). |
| **CPRD (Clinical Practice Research Datalink)** | Longitudinal primary care dataset from the UK, covering over 60 million patient records with coded diagnoses, prescriptions, and demographic data. | • **Primary care/outpatient focus**; lacks high-frequency intra-hospital time-series measurements (vitals/labs) found in MIMIC.<br>• Heavily gated with strict data access requirements. |
| **n2c2 (National NLP Clinical Challenges)** | Publicly available benchmark datasets derived from real clinical notes across various challenge years. Includes tasks like Named Entity Recognition (NER), relation extraction, clinical concept extraction, and coreference resolution. | • Focuses almost exclusively on **unstructured clinical text** parsing rather than tabular relational EHR databases.<br>• Highly curated and human-annotated, but small in scale relative to full EHR repositories. |
| **Highmark / Clinical NLP Extract Benchmarks** | Specialty-specific clinical extraction datasets (e.g., Cardiology, Oncology notes) used to evaluate LLMs on structured parameter extraction from progress notes. | • Captures real-world outpatient/inpatient notes beyond ICU settings.<br>• Notes often contain informal clinical jargon, shorthand, and template artifacts. |
